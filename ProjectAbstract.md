# Project Abstract

**Project Title:** School Automation System — TechVeda

**Abstract:** School management in Indian educational institutions continues to rely on fragmented manual processes — attendance on paper registers, fee records in ledger books, exam marks in spreadsheets, and timetables through hours of manual coordination — creating operational inefficiency, data inaccuracy, and significant administrative burden. While standalone digital tools exist, they remain disconnected and fail to provide a unified platform serving all stakeholders from a single application. This project presents a **School Automation System — TechVeda** that combines role-based web portals with AI-powered automation to deliver a comprehensive school management solution. The system provides three dashboards: an administrative portal for managing teachers, students, fees, timetables, attendance, and notices; a teacher portal for attendance marking, marks entry, AI-powered exam generation, and notice posting; and a student portal for viewing academic records, taking live examinations, paying fees online, and downloading PDF reports. The platform introduces intelligent automation through an AI-powered timetable generator ensuring conflict-free scheduling and balanced teacher workloads, and an AI-powered examination engine that extracts content from uploaded PDFs and creates MCQ assessments using Groq AI (LLaMA 3.3). Online fee payment is integrated through Razorpay, and automated email notifications are delivered via Nodemailer. The application is developed using React/Vite for the frontend, Express.js for the backend, MongoDB with Mongoose for the database, and JWT-based authentication with role-based authorization. By consolidating all core school operations into a single modular web application with AI-assisted automation, the system aims to reduce administrative complexity, improve data accuracy, and make school management more efficient, transparent, and scalable.

**Application Area:**

1. **Educational Institutions:** Enables schools to manage students, teachers, attendance, fees, exams, timetables, and notices through a unified web platform with role-based access control.

2. **School Administration and Fee Management:** Supports administrators in tracking fee payments, generating records, and processing online payments through Razorpay with complete audit trails.

**Impact on Industry:**

1. **Operational Efficiency and Automation:** Replaces fragmented manual processes with AI-powered timetable generation and automated exam creation, reducing administrative workload and human errors.

2. **Digital Transformation in Education:** Demonstrates how full-stack web engineering, AI integration, and online payment processing can modernize school management and improve stakeholder communication.

**Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):** SDG 4 – Quality Education; SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure.

**Technology Stack –** React/Vite, Express.js, MongoDB/Mongoose, JWT, Groq AI (LLaMA 3.3), Razorpay, Nodemailer, jsPDF, Axios, and Node.js.
